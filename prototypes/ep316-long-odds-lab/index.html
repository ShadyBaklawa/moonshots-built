Why doesn't 60% ÷ 14% = 8x?

Dave's AI said foreign-born founders are 8x more likely to be a top tech CEO. But 60% ÷ 14% is about 4.3x — a representation ratio, not the 8x stated on the show. A few things could explain the gap, and the tool doesn't pick one for you:

  • Different kind of ratio. The odds ratio for these same two numbers — (60/40) ÷ (14/86) — comes out to about 9.2x, much closer to the stated 8x than the simple 4.3x representation ratio is. "X times more likely" is easy to say about either one, and they're genuinely different quantities.
  • Different CEO set or year. The AI's "top tech CEOs by market cap" sample may not be exactly the same list, cutoff, or point in time used to produce the 60% figure quoted on the show.
  • Loose wording. "Times more likely" in conversation doesn't always map onto one specific formula — it can blend ratio, odds ratio, and rough intuition.
  • Small-sample noise. As the band below shows, "60% of top CEOs" estimated from a handful of people carries real uncertainty, so the point estimate itself has wiggle room.
