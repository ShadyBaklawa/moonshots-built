Thought experiment, not a product. This assumes a neutrino beam could be aimed precisely enough to punch straight through the Earth and be caught on the other side — detecting a handful of neutrinos at all requires buildings full of equipment today, let alone a steerable point-to-point link. The animation is slowed down roughly —× so the race is visible; real transit times are listed above. Distances and light-speed latency are computed exactly from the two cities' coordinates — everything else about building this is speculation from the episode.
