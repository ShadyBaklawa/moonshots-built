How the score works

Each row scores 0–10 from three bundled rules, shown here so nothing is a black box:

  • +5 if it is not signed or documented anywhere.
  • +4 if there is no backup person who could step in.
  • +1 if annual revenue at risk is $100,000 or more (scales up to +1 per 10 rows ranked by revenue, see ranking).

Rows are ranked by score first, then by revenue at risk. The "two week exposure" total adds the revenue at risk of every row with no backup person, since that is what stalls the moment one person is unreachable. This is a simple heuristic, not a legal or financial assessment — for actual contracts, talk to a lawyer.
