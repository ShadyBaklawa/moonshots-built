Egress Sniff Sheet

For lab managers and PIs: paste your router, firewall or DNS export and see exactly which devices are talking to the outside world — how much, to where, and at what hour.

🔒 Everything below runs in this browser tab. Your log is parsed on your device and is never uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.
