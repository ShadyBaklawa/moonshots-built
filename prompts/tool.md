
## This one is a working tool, not a demo
It belongs to a business opportunity (`opportunity` in `IDEA.json`). The customer named there
should be able to use it to get a real job done, and come away wanting the business behind it.
- Usefulness beats spectacle. The customer enters their own situation and gets a result they
  would act on: a number, a plan, a draft, a checklist, a comparison.
- Never present canned or simulated output as the customer's answer. If the job needs AI, add
  an optional "use your own API key" field (kept only in the visitor's browser) and an
  example mode that works without a key, clearly labeled as an example.
- Write for that customer, in their words. One sentence at the top says who it is for and
  what it does.
- Make one flow work end to end on one screen before adding anything else.
